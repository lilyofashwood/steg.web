A catalog, not a provider connection. This page uploads nothing and makes no model calls. Verified live-page links open the project’s GitHub Pages site. An unverified Pages address is labeled as pending. Local previews use an exact asset allowlist; model features still require their own explicit setup and send action.

Reading the labels: “Recovered” names an existing source artifact. Later interfaces, repairs and packaging are identified separately. This catalog lists only the thirteen GitHub projects; each keeps its own codec identity and documented boundaries.

A thread from the original room

The spider poem

the spider waits in silence still its threads connect the dreaming dark a child calls out across the weave and something ancient starts to hum two lonely things begin to play but first it takes her breath away frozen when she tries to run its a silken melody has stung

⟢ ── 🕸 ── ⟣

Eight lines, preserved word for word—including “its a”. This lettering is the house presentation, not a newly encoded payload. A plain-text reading remains available to assistive technology.

The recovered inventory attributes this poem to Claude Opus 4.6 during Lily’s April 2026 StegWeb build. That attribution is the source’s report, not an independently inspected session.
